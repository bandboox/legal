Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

BandBook ("we", "our", or "us") operates the BandBook mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using BandBook, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and profile details you provide when creating an account.
  • Profile information: Your role (artist, venue, or sponsor), biography, genre, photos, and other details you add to your profile.
  • Location data: With your permission, we use your approximate location to show nearby artists and venues. We do not store your precise location on our servers.
  • Messages: Content of messages sent between users through the in-app messaging feature.
  • Booking information: Details of gig requests, offers, and booking agreements made through the App.
  • Push notification tokens: Device tokens used to deliver push notifications. These are associated with your account.
  • Usage data: Basic analytics about how you interact with the App (e.g., screens visited) to help us improve the product.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the BandBook service
  • Connect artists, venues, and sponsors with each other
  • Send push notifications about booking requests, messages, and offers
  • Show relevant nearby listings based on your location
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other users: Your public profile information (name, role, bio, photos) is visible to other BandBook users.
  • Service providers: We use Supabase for database and authentication services, and Expo for push notification delivery. These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually bound to protect it.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of BandBook and its users.

5. Push Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications to inform you of new messages, booking requests, and offers. You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's Settings app.

6. Location Data

We request access to your location only while the App is in use, and only to show you nearby artists and venues. You can revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings app. We do not share your location data with third parties or store precise location history on our servers.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data (including your profile, messages, and bookings) is permanently deleted from our systems. See Section 8 for how to delete your account.

8. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your BandBook account at any time from within the App:

  • Go to Profile → Delete Account
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. All your profile data, messages, bookings, and associated records will be removed from our systems.

9. Children's Privacy

BandBook is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and row-level security on our database. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-Party Sign-In

You may sign in using Apple or Google. When you do, we receive only the information those services share with us (typically your name and email address). Your use of Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In is also governed by Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the App or by other means. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
